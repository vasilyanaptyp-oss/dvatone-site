01About Dvatone

Colour is a feeling, not a code

Dvatone makes multicolour decorative coatings for interiors. A sample shows a colour, but it cannot tell you how that colour will play with the light in your home. So we show depth, texture and the subtle shifts of tone.

We are based in Kyiv. The coating is made to the volume you need, from 2 l.
