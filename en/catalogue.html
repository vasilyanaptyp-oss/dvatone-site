01The colour fan

Coating samples in one fan

The Dvatone fan is made of strips with real coating on them. Each one shows how the granules sit on the base tone and how deep the colour is.

It is a working tool for designers, architects and homeowners: place it against a wall, a floor or furniture and compare.

The fan comes in a case that protects the samples.

02How to use it

Four steps from fan to composition

  1. 01

    Open the fan

    Look through the strips from light to dark and find the tones close to your idea.

  2. 02

    Hold it up to the room

    Hold a strip against the wall, floor and furniture. Look at it in the morning, at midday and in the evening.

  3. 03

    Find the code in the catalogue

    Enter the shade code in the search below to see it in the digital catalogue and add it to a composition.

  4. 04

    Build a composition

    Combine several shades in the generator and get a calculation for your area.
