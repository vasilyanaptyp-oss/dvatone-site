01What this page covers

This page briefly describes what data you share on the Dvatone site and why we need it. If you need a clarification, write to us on Telegram: @Dvatone_bot.

02Data you enter in forms

  • Designer Box request: full name, studio or company, role, email, phone or Telegram, delivery city and address, project description.
  • Application request: name, phone, email or Telegram, site city, approximate area, shade or composition, site description.
  • The form in Contacts: name, phone, email or Telegram, topic and message.

03Why we need them

To contact you about your request: to confirm shades, delivery, the site or your question.

04Consent

Request forms include a consent box for processing your data so that we can contact you about the request. Without it the form is not sent.
