01Technology and origin

What is Dvatone multicolour paint, and how is it related to Dialcolor?

The base components of Dialcolor, the legendary multicolour coating, are made in Belgium by Trimetal, part of the global chemicals group AkzoNobel. Dvatone is our own system for selecting and making unique multicolour compositions. Original Belgian Dialcolor components give it a reliable technological base, and our own innovative ingredients improve the paint’s performance, heighten its decorative qualities and create entirely new visual effects, such as a refined pearlescent sheen.

What is the main advantage of Dvatone over ordinary interior paints?

The main difference is a unique multicolour mosaic texture that forms as the paint goes on. Thanks to our own modifiers, the paint builds a strong three-dimensional structure: it looks deeper and richer than a classic single-colour finish and is far more resistant to wear and tear.

02Colour and ordering

Who designs the colour compositions in the Dvatone catalogue?

Our signature catalogue is not just a set of random multicolour compositions: it is the result of working with well-known Ukrainian designers. Their recommendations on combining colours became the basis on which we created the current collection of premium coatings, available to order.

Where are the paints from your catalogue already used?

Dvatone colour solutions and materials are recognised internationally. Textures from our core catalogue already decorate landmark buildings in the USA, Europe and the UAE. This confirms that the coating meets the highest international standards of design and performance.

Which colour standards can a multicolour composition be ordered to?

Our laboratory can develop and prepare any custom multicolour composition. We reproduce shades precisely to the most widely used colour standards: NCS, RAL and Sikkens 5051.

What is the smallest amount of Dvatone paint I can order for a project?

The minimum custom order is 2 litres, and above that the volume goes up in 1-litre steps. Architects and contractors can work out exactly how much material they need, with no wasted budget.

03Durability and safety

Is Dvatone paint suitable for spaces that see heavy use?

Yes, the coating is ideal for private interiors and commercial spaces alike. Dvatone paint is highly wear-resistant, washes very well and stands up to regular wet cleaning, keeping its original colour brightness and delicate texture for many years.

What if the wall gets damaged by accident?

One of the material’s key advantages is local repair. If scratches or chips appear, the damaged spot can be restored on its own. Once dry, the repair layer blends completely into the texture of the wall, with no visible seams or patches, so there is no need to repaint the whole surface from corner to corner.

Does the paint give off a toxic smell while it is applied and while it dries?

No. Dvatone paint is made on an eco-⁠friendly water base and has no odour. Its volatile organic compound (VOC) content is below 30 g/⁠l, so it is safe to use in enclosed rooms, children’s rooms and buildings with strict environmental requirements.

04Application and equipment

What equipment works best for applying multicolour paint?

For a flawless signature finish, and for the multicolour granules to open up properly, the best choice is a Rigo professional compressor (Italy) with a 2.5 mm nozzle. The application technique itself is designed to make the work quick and easy.

How long does it take to get the material in Ukraine?

Base components and ingredients for production are always in stock in Ukraine. This keeps tinting and dispatch times to a minimum and helps avoid long delays on building sites.

05Service, training and delivery

Do you support applicators who are using your material for the first time?

Yes. We provide full technical support and train your staff, or the decorators themselves, on site. It matters to us that the application technique is followed to the letter.

How do I get the Dvatone signature catalogue of colour compositions?

Every customer who buys our paint receives the catalogue free of charge. It is a professional tool that helps designers and clients work with the palette comfortably, right on site.

Can you apply the paint for us on a turnkey basis?

Yes. We offer a full range of services and carry out the contracting work ourselves. Our professional team has many years of experience with this material and is ready to deliver a project of any complexity, with a guarantee on the quality of application.

Can you deliver Dvatone paint outside Ukraine?

Yes. We have set up logistics and ship the paint to any country in the world. Delivery details, customs documents and transport terms are agreed with each customer individually, for the specific project.

06Good to know

How much paint will I need?

Base consumption starts at 200 ml/⁠m². Enter your area in the composition generator for an estimate; we will confirm the exact volume after your request.

Where can I find the requirements for the substrate and wall preparation?

They are in the technical data sheets (TDS), available in the Dvatone Telegram bot.

How close is the colour on screen to the real one?

A screen shows the character of the texture and the relationship between shades, but every display renders colour a little differently. For the final choice, use the Dvatone colour fan or a Designer Box and judge the shade in the light of your space.

How do I get samples and find out the price?

For designers and architects there is the Designer Box: send a request on its page. The current price list is available in the Dvatone Telegram bot.
