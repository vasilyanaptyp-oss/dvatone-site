Ask a question

01The coating

What is a multicolour coating?

It is a water-based decorative coating in which granules of several colours sit on a base tone. In Dvatone the granule fraction is very fine, so the texture is refined and even, never blotchy. The surface looks different depending on the light and the distance.

What can you tell me about the composition and safety?

The coating is made on a water base. Its VOC content is below 30 g/l and it has no odour. It is made with European technology and raw materials from the AkzoNobel group (Trimetal).

How durable is it, and does the wall still breathe?

The coating is wear-resistant and breathable, so the wall keeps its ability to let vapour pass. It is also optically stable.

Can I repair a single spot?

Yes. The coating allows seamless local repair: a damaged area is restored on its own, without repainting the whole wall.

02Application

Can I order exactly the amount I need?

Yes. We make the paint to your volume, from 2 l (2, 3, 4, 5 ... 15 l). That helps you avoid buying more than you need.

How much coating do I need?

Base consumption starts at 200 ml/m². You can enter the area in the composition generator, and it will work out the volume you need.

Which substrates is it suitable for, and how should they be prepared?

General guidance is in the technical data sheets (TDS), available in the Dvatone Telegram bot.

What is used to apply the coating? Do I need a compressor?

Application uses compressor equipment, including a Rigo compressor, and tools matched to the material. If you would rather have specialists do it, there is a professional application service.

What is professional application (expert supervision)?

It is a service in which a Dvatone coating is applied professionally, so that the result matches the chosen shade and texture.

03Generator and ordering

How close is the colour on screen to the real one?

A screen shows the character of the texture and the relationship between shades, but every display renders colour a little differently. For the final choice, use the Dvatone colour fan or a Designer Box and judge the shade in the light of your space.

What does the composition generator do?

You combine shades from the catalogue, set proportions and fraction, and see the composition on a neutral backing, so its colour never depends on the page background. Enter the area in m² to get a volume calculation. From the generator you can download a colour passport as a PDF (litres, binders, bases and component proportions) and a seamless texture for 3ds Max.

How do I get samples and find out the price?

For designers and architects there is the Designer Box: send a request on its page. The current price list is available in the Dvatone Telegram bot.
