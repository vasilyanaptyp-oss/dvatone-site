01What expert supervision means

A professional application service

Expert supervision means a Dvatone coating is applied professionally, so that the result matches the chosen shade and texture. A consultant will explain how application can be arranged on your site and what the service includes.

  • An even, calm texture without blotches
  • The colour on the wall matches the chosen shade
  • The right tools and the right working method

02Tools and equipment

Tools matched to the material

Application uses compressor equipment, including a Rigo compressor, and tools matched to the material. Ask in the FAQ or in the Telegram bot for details.
