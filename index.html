How to get the multicolour effect on the wall

The colours are not applied one at a time: the multicolour texture forms as the paint is sprayed on.

  1. 01

    Preparing the wall

    The wall is prepared for application. The substrate and preparation requirements are in the technical data sheets (TDS) in the Dvatone Telegram bot.

  2. 02

    Spray application

    The paint is sprayed on with a compressor. The best results come from Rigo professional compressors (Italy) with a 2.5 mm nozzle.

  3. 03

    Dvatone on the wall

    Granules of several colours open up on the base tone and form a strong three-dimensional structure.

08FAQ

Questions and answers

Brief answers to what designers and homeowners ask most often.

All questions
What is the main advantage of Dvatone over ordinary interior paints?

The main difference is a unique multicolour texture that forms as the paint goes on. Thanks to an innovative paint preparation method that achieves the fine-granule result, the coating builds a strong three-dimensional structure: it looks deeper and richer than a classic single-colour finish and is far more resistant to wear and tear.

What is the smallest amount of Dvatone paint I can order for a project?

The minimum custom order is 2 litres, and above that the volume goes up in 1-litre steps. Architects and contractors can work out exactly how much material they need, with no wasted budget.

What if the wall gets damaged by accident?

One of the material’s key advantages is local repair. If scratches or chips appear, the damaged spot can be restored on its own. Once dry, the repair layer blends completely into the texture of the wall, with no visible seams or patches, so there is no need to repaint the whole surface from corner to corner.
